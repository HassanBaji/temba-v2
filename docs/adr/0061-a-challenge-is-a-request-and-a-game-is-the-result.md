# A Challenge is a request and a Game is the result

**Status:** proposed (draft for the owner's review). The request half landed with posting and the Open board; the Game half landed with the Challenge Game. Amends ADR-0035 for this one Game kind. Amended by [ADR-0069](./0069-a-football-level-is-an-ovr-moved-by-four-parts.md) makes a football Challenge Game count for Temba Level.

A Team on Temba has a record it cannot earn: nothing lets it find another Team and play. The owner wants a board where a Team posts what it is looking for, another Team answers with a time and a Venue, and the poster confirms one answer. The question is what the board holds: a Game that waits for an opponent, or something that is not a Game until both Teams agree.

## Decision

- **A Challenge is its own record, never a Game.** `challenges` holds the posting Team, its sport, the Challenge format (`side_size`, football only, 5, 6 or 7), Level wanted (`level_min_tenths`, `level_max_tenths`, the same units as a Game Level range), preferred Venues (`challenge_venues`, ordered), days, timings and optional exact slots, a line of up to 140 characters, and `duration_minutes` (padel 90, football 60), which becomes the Game window. Nothing about who will play: a football Roster may be up to twenty and the players of one Game are named after it exists (ADR-0060).
- **Posting.** Someone who acts for the Team (ADR-0059) posts. Football needs a Format the Roster can field (`teamCanPostFormat`); padel has no Format and needs both players. Venues must offer the sport and exact slots must be ahead. A Team has at most one open Challenge, enforced by the partial unique index `challenges_team_open_uq` and checked by the door. Withdrawing frees the Team to post again.
- **Who may answer.** Any other Team of the sport whose Roster holds at least the Format's side (padel: both players), that shares no member with the poster, and whose Team Level sits inside Level wanted when one is set. A Team with no rated member has no Team Level and is outside any set range. There is no request or waiver path. The Open board is visible to every signed-in User; a viewer with no such Team sees the card greyed with the reason (`challenges.listOpen`, `challenges.byId`).
- **A Challenge expires; it is checked on read and stamped by a job.** Open for seven days. `@repo/domain/challenge-clock` answers whether a row is still open, and every read applies it, so a row the job has not stamped never acts. A Graphile Worker job (`challenge.expire`) enqueued at `expires_at` with `mode: "replace"` stamps `expired` and tells the Users who act for the Team (`challenge_expired`, once per Challenge by its dedupe key). A Challenge never expires while a Proposal is pending: the job re-schedules itself at that Proposal's expiry. Expired and withdrawn Challenges leave the board quietly. Precedent: `settleMvpJob` and the Invite link check-on-read.
- **Proposals are stored beside it.** `challenge_proposals` (a Venue, a start, an optional price per player, 48 hours to answer; one pending per Team per Challenge) exists from the start so the clock can see them.
- **Confirming one Proposal creates the Challenge Game, in one transaction.** `challenges.confirmProposal` locks the Challenge and the Proposal `for update`, then reads every propose rule again for both Teams (a member may have left; the start must still be an hour ahead); a failure names the reason and writes nothing. Then it creates a Friendly game with `group_id` null, `games.challenge_id` set, `registration_closed_at = now`, the Proposal's Venue, Court and price, and a window of `duration_minutes`; both Game teams carry `team_id`, the poster's on side 1. The Challenge becomes `confirmed` with `confirmed_proposal_id` and `game_id`; every other pending Proposal is `passed_over` and its Team told (`challenge_proposal_passed_over`); the expiry jobs are removed; `challenge_proposal_confirmed` goes to both Teams except the confirmer.
- **Padel is seated at confirm.** Through the shared Friendly Game create module, then both pairs are seated through the seat internals, Positions from Preferred Position (a picked side first, Either takes what is left, two Either players go left then right). Every member of both Teams is told.
- **Football opens empty.** Through `createFootballGame` with the Challenge format as Side size and `players_allowed = 2 × (side + 3)`; then two empty sides in black and white, the one Match with both slots, and its Score Set shell. Nobody is seated until a Captain names the Matchday squad (ADR-0060), so only who acts for each Team is told. A `challenge.squad-due` job at kick-off − 24 h reminds whoever acts for a side still empty or short.
- **Organizers are whoever acts for either Team.** `isGameOrganizer` and `gameAdminUserIds` branch on `challenge_id` to the acting Users of both Teams; `canViewGame` admits any Roster member of either Team, named or not. Cancel, score, confirm, name, window and price work for them as on any Game. Admit, Waitlist, Invite doors, add by phone or from the Group, leave, kick, publish sides and shape edits (caps, Game Level range, Season) are refused: "Teams are set by the challenge; captains name the squads" (`requireNotChallengeGame`).
- **Rating (amends ADR-0035).** ADR-0035 rates a Game only on a Verified Group, so a made-up Group cannot move Levels. A padel Challenge Game has two independent Teams who each confirm the result, a stronger check than one Group's word, so it is Rated (`rated = true`, `group_rated = false`) at the existing groupless weight of 0.5. A football Challenge Game stays Unrated, like every football Game.
- **The Challenge stays `confirmed`** whatever happens to that Game, cancellation included; a new Challenge is a new post.

**Considered options**: an open Game with one Team seated and the other side empty (every Game reader, admit door and Notification would have to learn a Game with no date, no Venue and no opponent, and a Game cannot have three Venues or "weekends, evenings"); a Challenge that never expires (the board fills with stale requests); expiry by job only (a late job leaves a stale card actionable) or on read only (nobody is told).

## Consequences

- The `Game` glossary entry gains a second groupless kind beside the Booking Game, the Challenge Game, and `Organizer`, `Game admit` and `Invite doors` say what it refuses.
- `games` gains `challenge_id`; `notifications` gains `challenge_proposal_confirmed`, `challenge_proposal_passed_over` and `challenge_squad_due`.
- Mine's "Accepted, now games" and "Played" read `games.challenge_id` for every Team the viewer is on; the Games hub marks the row CHALLENGE and offers no join.
- Two colluding padel Teams can move Levels at half weight; the wrong-score report and both Teams' confirmations are the check.
- `notifications` gains `challenge_id` and the `challenge_expired` type; the Web App and the Notification email send it to Challenges › Mine.
- `@repo/domain` gains `challenge-clock`, `challenge-card` and `challenge-form`, read by both client Apps and the API.
- The Mine segment lists the viewer's Teams' open Challenges; Proposals and Challenge Games join it with their doors.
