# Padel Levels were recalculated once under the declared-band rules

**Status:** accepted. Spec: `.scratch/padel-declared-band-trust/spec.md`, section 9. Amends [ADR-0070](./0070-a-padel-level-weighs-the-partner-and-reads-from-one.md) ("New Matches only"): this is the one replay. Amends [ADR-0054](./0054-a-notification-email-is-a-job-on-the-notification-row.md): its Notification is written already emailed, with no email Job.

ADR-0096 made a padel declaration set the starting φ, and ADR-0097 made a declared C or above lose more at its own level until confirmed. Both apply only from their deploy, so every existing player would keep a Level built under the old rules beside new players built under the new ones. The owner chose to bring existing players in line with a full replay of history.

## Decision

- **Every padel Rating is recomputed from the start, once**, as if ADR-0096 and ADR-0097 had always been in force, and the stored numbers are rewritten to match: each player's μ, φ, σ, band, `last_rated_at` and recovered Declared band, and the before and after of every padel Rated Match, Place award and Level override row.
- **Today's formula for every Match**, including Matches rated before ADR-0070. Older formulas are not applied by date. The report's baseline pass (φ 350, no Prove-it rule) says how much of each move comes from the formula reaching old Matches and how much from the new rules.
- **The ledger is the input.** A Match is replayed if and only if it has padel `rating_events` rows today. Each player starts from their stored Declared band, which never changes (ADR-0096); only a Rating with none has it read back from the first row of their history, and μ starts from that row either way. Where the ledger does not hold enough, a stated rule applies and the report lists the row under the rule's name: a start μ that is no band midpoint is kept and its band becomes the Declared band (`start-mu-off-midpoint`, RQ-5); a seat whose history an Unclaimed player merge deleted plays as a Guest player (`seat-without-event`, RQ-6); a row that cannot be recomputed is carried over by its stored move (`carried-over`, RQ-7), and the apply needs `--carry-unplaced` to proceed; a stored Declared band that differs from the one the history would recover is kept (`declared-band-mismatch`). The Reliability loss factor of an event older than ADR-0097 is derived once from `reliability_part` and stored.
- **Kept as they were**: which Matches counted, who won, every Game, Match, Set and Standing, who is confirmed, Reliability, every football Rating and Group Tier, and the Level each Level override set (μ and band; its φ is re-capped from the replayed φ).
- **Closed Seasons are re-read.** Their By level tables and Most improved are derived from the ledger on every read and nothing was stored at close, so they follow the recalculated values. Places and points never read a Level.
- **One transaction under a table lock.** The apply takes `LOCK TABLE ratings IN EXCLUSIVE MODE` before it reads, so every rating writer waits and is then rated on the recalculated values; reads are not blocked. It writes only rows whose values differ, copying each one whole into `rating_replay_backups` under a `rating_replay_runs` row first, beside the values it writes over it. A second run finds no difference and records nothing. `--restore <run id>` writes back only the columns the run wrote, and refuses once a later run exists, a padel ledger row was written or removed after the run, or a column the run wrote no longer holds what it wrote. A column the run never writes, such as Reliability, may move after the run without ending the restore, and the restore leaves it as it is.
- **Players are told in the app only.** In the apply transaction each account holder whose padel Level changes on its two-decimal face, or whose band letter changes, gets one `level_recalculated` Notification. `notify()` takes an `emailedAt` and writes those rows as already emailed, so no Notification email Job is enqueued and no email goes out. It is called for at most 1,000 players at a time, to stay inside Postgres's parameter limit, under one dedupe key per run.
- **It runs once**, after the Prove-it rule deploys and before the Level check does. It is not run again: the engine does not replay a Level check.

**Considered Options**: leaving existing players under the old rules (two populations on one scale); a φ-only backfill from the Declared band (the owner chose the full replay knowing it was not the planner's recommendation); applying older formulas by date (the Workspace does not hold the date ADR-0070 reached production); a resumable, batched replay (Glicko-2 is sequential, so a half-applied replay matches neither rule set, and it would have to block rating writes for its whole length anyway); appending a "recalculated" line to Rating history instead of rewriting it (every later line would still read the old moves); a snapshot of closed Seasons' levels (nothing reads one today, and the owner accepted that they change).

## Consequences

- Levels players have already seen change with no Match behind the change, and players whose own rules did not change move too, because everyone they played did.
- Rating history is rewritten, not appended: every past Match line shows the recalculated move, and the old numbers are only in the backup.
- Notification emails, shared Result cards and shared Standing images already sent keep the old numbers.
- Rating writes wait while the apply runs; the dry run's load and compute time is the lower bound for the lock.
- The backup can be written back only until the next padel Rated Match, Level override or Place award, or until anything else writes over a value the run wrote. A Reliability step does not end it.
- The recalculation's Notification never reaches an inbox, unlike every other Notification type (ADR-0054).
