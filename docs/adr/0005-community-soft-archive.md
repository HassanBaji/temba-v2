# Soft-archive Communities instead of deleting them

**Status:** superseded by [ADR-0055](./0055-community-is-retired-and-a-group-links-to-a-venue.md). Community is retired; Soft-archive is a Venue rule only.

Archiving a Community hides it and its Club Groups together. Members still see history and Games. Club Groups stay attached; they do not become Loose Groups. Hard-delete would cascade through Groups into Games, and leaving is not archive. Unarchive restores listing and join rules. Invite consume is refused while archived; tokens are kept unless staff rotate.
