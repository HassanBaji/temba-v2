# 07: Club Group Private invites and leave Community

**What to build:** Owner, Admin, or that Club Group’s creator invite an existing Community member into a Club Group Private in-app (no email, no Invite link). Others cannot. Leaving the Community removes the User from all of its Club Groups and does not touch Loose Groups or other Communities. A User can belong to two Communities at once.

**Blocked by:** 05: Club Group Public: create, join, leave Group

**Status:** ready-for-agent

**Parent:** #1 Community: clubs, Groups, sports, and Private invites

- [ ] Owner or Admin can create a Club Group Private with one sport on Community sports
- [ ] Owner, Admin, or that Group’s creator can invite an existing Community member into that Club Group Private in-app
- [ ] A Community Member who is not staff and not that Group’s creator cannot invite themselves or others
- [ ] Invitee must already be a Community member; outsiders cannot join via this path
- [ ] Club Group Private has no Email invite and no Invite link
- [ ] Leave Community removes membership in all of that Community’s Club Groups
- [ ] Leave Community does not touch Loose Groups or memberships in other Communities
- [ ] A User can belong to two Communities at once
