# 09: Owner roles

**What to build:** An Owner promotes a Member or Admin to Owner and demotes another Owner. Multiple Owners are allowed. Admins cannot change Owner-ship. The last Owner cannot leave or self-demote. Leaving is not Soft-archive.

**Blocked by:** 03: Request to join Community Public

**Status:** ready-for-agent

**Parent:** #1 Community: clubs, Groups, sports, and Private invites

- [ ] An Owner can promote a Member or Admin to Owner
- [ ] An Owner can demote another Owner to Admin or Member
- [ ] Multiple Owners are allowed
- [ ] An Admin cannot promote, demote, or change Owner-ship (including promoting themselves)
- [ ] The last Owner cannot leave or self-demote until they promote someone else
- [ ] Refusing last-Owner leave does not Soft-archive the Community
