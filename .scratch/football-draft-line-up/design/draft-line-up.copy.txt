Only organizers, admins and the picked captains see a draft, and it can be shared as an image in the style of the result cards. Captain pick is a third way to make the teams: two captains pick in turn, in a room they share.

DRAFT AND CAPTAINS
Save draft
Sits beside Publish teams on the existing Make teams screen. A draft keeps the two squads, the bib colours and the captains, and nothing reaches the players.
Captains
One per team, picked from that team’s players, or none. A C on their picture marks them everywhere the teams are shown. If a captain is moved to the other team, the pick is cleared.
The draft on the game
Once saved, the organizer’s game screen shows the draft teams with who has answered: approved, waiting, or asked for a change. Publish, Share and Edit sit under it.
Approval does not block
The organizer can publish at any time. Changing a squad after its captain approved asks that captain again.

WHO SEES IT
Who can see a draft
Organizers and admins of the group, and the captains picked on it. With no captains picked, only organizers and admins. Nobody else, until it is published.
What a captain does

Same screen as Share result

Black, like the result cards. Names, positions and captains only: no levels or averages leave the app. Captains carry a C. Every card is stamped Draft, not final.

CAPTAIN PICK
A third way
Captain pick sits next to By level and Manual on Make teams. The organizer names a captain for each side and how the first pick is decided: a toss, or one side.
The pick room
Both captains are told, and the room opens from that notification or from the game. Nothing starts until both are in; the one who arrives first sees the other as not here yet and can remind them.
One pick each, in turn
The captain on turn taps a player and confirms. The player moves to their side with the pick number and the other captain sees it at once. Then it is the other’s turn.
What the captains see
Their two sides filling up, the average level of each, and the pool with position and level, best level first.
The organizer
Watches the same room without picking. Can pick for a captain who has dropped out, undo the last pick, or stop and finish the teams by hand.
When it ends
The teams land where a saved draft does, with both captains already agreed. The organizer publishes, shares or edits from there. Players see nothing until then.
What I assumed
No timer on a pick. With an odd number the last player goes to the side that is short. Keepers are picked like anyone else, marked GK.
