A SERIES
What it is
A social or a game the group plays again and again, with a name and a badge. Each time it is played is an edition, numbered on its own.
Where it lives
A fourth tab on the group, next to Standing. Running series first, ended ones under them. Everyone in the group sees the tab; only admins and organizers see New series.
The badge
A six-sided coin, so it never reads as a mark, which is round. The admin picks one of seven emblems and one of six colours. No uploads, so every badge stays in the Temba style.
Verified groups only
The Series tab shows only in verified groups. Other groups have no tab, no picker and no mention of series anywhere.
Ending one
From the series menu. Nothing can be attached to it any more, and the badges already won stay on profiles.
02Attaching it. One choice on the last step of creating a social or a game; then it shows on the game for everyone.

ATTACH
One optional step
Series sits on the review step of Create, above who can enter. It lists the group’s running series and says which edition this would be.
Only where it applies
The picker shows only when the game is in a verified group that has a series. Otherwise the step looks as it does today.
On the game
The lifted card carries the badge, the name and the edition. A plain card under it says what winning it means and links to the series.
Changing it
The organizer can add, change or remove the series from Edit game until the score is in.

WINNING
Who wins
In a social, the team on top of the final table, so both players. In a game, every player on the winning side. A draw gives the edition to nobody.
When
The moment the last score is saved. If a score is corrected later, the series follows the corrected result.
The moment
The finished game opens on the winners with the badge, and says which number it is for each of them. Share uses the existing result cards.
The series page
The badge and the group, how many editions, who has won it most, and every edition with its winners. The next one is hatched until it is played.

PROFILE
A Series card
Above Marks. One badge per series the player has won, the count in the corner the way marks show theirs, the group under the name.
Both sports together
Series are not split by sport: a player’s padel and football series sit in the same card, most recent win first.
Latest
A new series win is an update on the profile and on Home, like a new mark.
Behind a badge
Tapping one lists the editions won, with the date and who it was won with, and leads to the series.
What I assumed
Wins are public on the profile like marks are. A player cannot hide a series.
